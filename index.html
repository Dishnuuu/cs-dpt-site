Computer Science is the study of systems.

We build real hardware, write production code, and defend original research before graduation.

CODE LOGIC ARCH SYS AI

From the HOD

A note from the head of department.

"Computer science is not about memorising syntax. It is about learning to ask precise questions, build systems that answer them, and stay curious when the answer is not obvious. Here, students do real work from the first semester: they wire up hardware, ship code, and defend original ideas. That is how an education becomes a career."

Dr. Meera Iyer · Head of Department
